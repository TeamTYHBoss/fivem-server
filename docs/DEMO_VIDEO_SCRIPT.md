# Fast Food Demo Video Script

## Video title

**Fast Food Restaurants — Counter Ordering, Food Bags, and Eating**

## Video length

About 60 seconds.

## Scene 1 — Restaurant exterior

**On-screen text:**

```text
Fast Food Restaurants
```

**Voiceover:**

"Players can now visit fast food restaurants across the city. Each restaurant has its own menu, cashier, and customers."

**Visuals:**

- Show the player walking toward a restaurant.
- Show the restaurant exterior.
- Show NPC customers inside.

## Scene 2 — Counter ordering NPCs

**On-screen text:**

```text
NPCs order at the counter
```

**Voiceover:**

"NPC customers order food at the counter every 15 seconds. One customer stands at the counter, while others sit and eat inside."

**Visuals:**

- Show the cashier NPC at the counter.
- Show the counter customer using the impatient scenario.
- Show seated customers eating.
- Show the order notification.

## Scene 3 — Viewing the menu

**On-screen text:**

```text
Press E to view the menu
```

**Voiceover:**

"Walk up to the counter and press E to view the available food and drinks."

**Visuals:**

- Show the player approaching the counter.
- Show the `[E] Order at [Restaurant]` prompt.
- Press E.
- Show the menu notification.

## Scene 4 — Buying food

**On-screen text:**

```text
Buy food with cash
```

**Voiceover:**

"Players can buy food with cash. The item is added to their inventory, and the purchase is logged."

**Visuals:**

- Show the player selecting a food item.
- Show the purchase notification.
- Show the item being added to inventory.

## Scene 5 — Cashier handover

**On-screen text:**

```text
Cashier hands over the food bag
```

**Voiceover:**

"After purchase, the cashier turns toward the player and hands over a food bag."

**Visuals:**

- Show the cashier turning toward the player.
- Show the food bag appearing.
- Show the food bag being handed over.

## Scene 6 — Player holds the bag

**On-screen text:**

```text
Player physically holds the bag
```

**Voiceover:**

"The player physically holds the food bag in their hand for 10 seconds."

**Visuals:**

- Show the food bag attached to the player’s hand.
- Show the player walking with the bag.
- Show the 10-second timer.

## Scene 7 — Eating from the bag

**On-screen text:**

```text
Press G to eat from the bag
```

**Voiceover:**

"While holding the bag, players can press G to eat the food directly from it. Eating removes the item from inventory and restores hunger."

**Visuals:**

- Show the `[G] Eat food from bag` prompt.
- Press G.
- Show the eating animation.
- Show the hunger restoration notification.
- Show the food bag disappearing.

## Scene 8 — Closing

**On-screen text:**

```text
Fast Food Restaurants
Counter ordering
Cashier handovers
Player food bags
Eat from the bag
```

**Voiceover:**

"Fast food restaurants now feel more alive, with counter ordering, cashier handovers, player-held food bags, and eating directly from the bag."

**Visuals:**

- Show the player walking away from the restaurant.
- Show the restaurant exterior one more time.
- Fade out.

## Recording checklist

- Record at 1080p or higher.
- Use smooth camera movement.
- Show the counter customer ordering.
- Show the cashier handover.
- Show the player holding the bag.
- Show the G eat interaction.
- Show the hunger restoration notification.
- Keep the video under 60 seconds.
- Add on-screen text for each feature.
- Use clear voiceover or background music.
