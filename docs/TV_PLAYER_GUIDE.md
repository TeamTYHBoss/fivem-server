# TV Player Guide

This guide explains how to use the TVs in the server.

## What you need

- Find the TV remote somewhere in the house.
- Access to a TV location.

## How to use a TV

1. Search the house for the TV remote.
2. When you get close, you will see a proximity hint.
3. Walk closer and press **E** to pick it up.
4. Walk close to a TV.
5. Press **E** to open the TV.
6. Use the channel list or arrow keys to change channels.
7. Press **Esc** to close the TV.

## TV remote

- The remote can spawn anywhere in the house.
- Players must search the house to find it.
- When close to the remote, players see **“TV remote nearby.”**
- When very close, players see **“[E] Pick up TV remote.”**
- The remote is required to use the TV.
- Once picked up, the remote is added to the player inventory.
- If the player leaves the house, the remote is dropped.
- The remote returns to a random hiding spot in the house.
- The server validates remote ownership.

## TV controls

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| E | Pick up remote / Watch TV |
| Right arrow | Next channel |
| Left arrow | Previous channel |
| Esc | Close TV |
| Click | Select channel |

## Channel selection

- The TV screen shows a channel list.
- Click a channel to select it.
- The selected channel is highlighted.
- The selected channel starts playing.
- You can also use the arrow keys to change channels.

## Last watched channel

- The TV remembers the last channel you watched.
- Each TV saves its own channel.
- When you return to the same TV, it opens on the channel you last watched.
- The channel is saved when you close the TV.

## TV locations

| TV | Location |
|---|---|
| Apartment TV | Apartment interior |
| House TV | House interior |

## Troubleshooting

- If you cannot open the TV, search the house for the remote.
- If you leave the house, the remote will drop and respawn.
- If a channel does not play, try another channel.
- If the TV does not respond, move away and come back.
- If the channel list is empty, try again later.

## Tips

- Search different areas of the house to find the remote.
- Watch for the “TV remote nearby” hint.
- Use the channel list to find a channel quickly.
- Use the arrow keys to flick through channels.
- Close the TV when you are done.
- Your last watched channel is saved for each TV.
